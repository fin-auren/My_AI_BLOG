India’s Data Centre Opportunity

India’s data centre industry is entering a period of rapid expansion. With current capacity at approximately 1.7 GW, the country’s data centre capacity is projected to grow to 7–9 GW by 2030.

However, the opportunity extends well beyond companies that directly own and operate data centres. The broader buildout is creating opportunities across power, infrastructure, construction, cooling, networking, equipment, and other supporting industries.

In this blog, we explore 20 stocks that are participating in India’s massive data centre buildout and examine how each company stands to benefit from this long-term investment cycle.

* **Engineering & Construction (EPC):** Larsen & Toubro (L&T) and KEC International (06:54-08:32).
